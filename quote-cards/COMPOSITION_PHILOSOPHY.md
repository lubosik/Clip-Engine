# Quiet Authority

The photograph remains the hero. The subject’s face, gesture, clothing, silhouette, and the objects that carry the scene’s meaning stay visually uninterrupted. The glass card belongs in genuine negative space whenever the frame provides it; when no empty region exists, it rests over the least descriptive area and remains as compact as the copy permits.

Glass should clarify hierarchy without becoming an opaque poster pasted over a photograph. Its hue follows the light and material already present in the frame, while its density is calibrated for immediate reading. The image continues through the frost, but never with enough detail to compete with the words. Every edge, shadow, and highlight is restrained and meticulously tuned.

Typography is sparse, crisp, and decisive. The quote carries the visual weight; the profile identity and follow line remain clearly readable but subordinate. Contrast is treated as a non-negotiable functional requirement, with solid ink values, controlled shadows, and an undisturbed field directly behind the copy.

Composition follows the subject rather than a universal lower-third habit. Panels may move to the upper field when John’s outfit or pose occupies the lower frame, while lower placement is reserved for images whose expressive information lives higher. The finished set should feel consistent because the judgment is consistent, not because every card occupies the same coordinates.

The final standard is master-level restraint: no added decoration, no unnecessary scale, and no element competing with the photograph. Each placement receives a second-pass inspection at both full size and grid size so the image reads first, the quote reads instantly, and neither weakens the other.
